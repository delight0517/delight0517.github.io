Good to know

Before you download.

Does this app need a campus account?

No. You can enter your timetable manually. Import is optional. Class Reminder is an independent app and is not affiliated with your university or another timetable service.

Do I need an AI API key?

No. Basic schedule features work without connecting an external AI service.

Is an Android version available?

We could not verify a public Google Play listing for India, so this page only links to the iPhone App Store version.

Where is my schedule stored?

Your basic timetable is stored on your device. See the privacy policy for details about optional cloud, AI and advertising services.

What does it cost?

The app is free to download. Confirm any current in-app purchase prices and terms on the India App Store page before purchase.

Which languages are available?

The app includes an English interface, but its current India App Store listing text is in Korean. An English listing is not yet available.
