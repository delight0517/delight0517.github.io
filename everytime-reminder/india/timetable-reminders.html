College timetable guide

Keep your classes, rooms and reminders in one place

College schedules change from day to day. A simple weekly view can help you check where you need to be and when your next class starts.

Start with the details you need

  1. Add each class with its weekday, start time and room. Include a lecturer name if it helps you recognise the class.
  2. Keep part-time shifts and personal commitments beside classes so you can spot clashes early.
  3. Set a reminder before classes where you need time to travel or prepare. Check that notifications are allowed in your phone settings.
  4. Review the week after a timetable change and update only the affected classes or dates.

When a timetable app can help

If your college portal shows the official timetable but you want a quick daily view and pre-class alerts, you can keep a separate personal copy. Everytime Reminder lets you enter classes manually; timetable import is optional. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with a college or campus service.
